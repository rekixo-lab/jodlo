# ADR-0011 — All-category coverage, smart discovery, no ten-category product cap

- **Decision:** Founder reconfirmed in the active JODLO chat on **2026-10-29** that JODLO must serve **all kinds of legitimate work** together, not launch as a ten-trade-only marketplace. This repeats the earlier broad coverage discussion recorded in [Smart Discovery](../reference/JODLO_SMART_DISCOVERY_FOUNDATION_2026-10-27.md).
- **Status:** **ACCEPTED product coverage objective** and engineering direction. It is **NOT** a category-specific safety/legality/wording acceptance, automatic activation of 889 legacy IDs, or permission to launch/deploy.
- **Source:** the pinned historic bilingual catalog has **28 Groups, 206 Families, 889 Categories, 7,267 Services**. The source inventory is a reference, not a trusted public seed.

## Binding decision

1. **No arbitrary ten-category limit.** Treat all **889 source Categories** as an inclusive discovery, editorial-review and potential worker-onboarding scope. Do not exclude a legitimate worker merely because the former ten-trade *review sample* omitted their job. The ten sample IDs in [the older proposal](../reference/JODLO_PILOT_TAXONOMY_PROPOSAL_2026-10-27.md) are a **nonbinding test/review subset**, not the first-launch allowlist, ranked default, geographic restriction or approval list. Do not ask the founder again to choose between ten versus all.
2. **Smart relevance rather than an 889-checkbox wall.** Workers should be able to find a matching service Category via bounded Hindi/English text Search, reviewed natural-language aliases, safe ambiguity choices, and hierarchical browsing as a fallback. Customers should see actual publicly eligible listings, with a distinct `availableOnly=1` facet. A first worker must also be able to find an active Category that has zero listings. Never auto-publish or auto-select an ambiguous trade; maintain explicit worker Category choice and current backend authorization.
3. **One full-coverage readiness program.** Process **every** original Category in the existing 45 offline review packets, preserve Group/Family lineage, and use one reproducible all-category source-to-JODLO mapping and activation review. The intent is broad coverage **together** when ready, not slow arbitrary selection of ten trades. Batch small *human review work* without treating the batches as public rollout restrictions. Avoid requiring the founder to manually select each of 889 IDs one by one: escalate actual exception/risk-policy decisions in a concise evidence-backed review.
4. **Safety exceptions remain real.** Historical `active`, `popular`, safety tiers, and old labels do not certify legality, worker qualifications or human-language quality. Some regulated or high-risk tasks need human/legal/safeguarding policy and may need verified qualifications, limited task scope or explicit nonactivation. Source defects include one incompatible Category ID, 616 Categories with mixed-script Hindi Service leaves, 127 Categories sharing a Service phrase with another Category, and some conflicting ancestor statuses. Never silently drop an ID; preserve each as a review item with a reason and corrective action. No unauthorized work should be made publishable merely to reach a numerical target.
5. **Already built versus not built:** All 889 source Categories and 7,267 Services are archived and source-checked; 889 synthetic keyset browse, global availability-aware active Category discovery, ambiguous result confirmation and 45 review packets exist. **No 889-category real D1 seed or complete reviewed service synonym index exists.** Existing Worker and Kotlin GET/POST routes operate only on currently active, server-authorized Categories. No new Group/Family API, live Android UI or location-based matching is implied.
6. **Existing independent founder gates:** M-005 category **breadth choice is DONE**, but risk eligibility, final IDs/mapping, editorial acceptance and initial contact/moderation policy remain pending; M-003 privacy/areas, M-004 visual design and M-007 production-only release are separate. Instant service self-publication in preapproved active Categories and no automatic verification (ADR-0009) remain unchanged. Only Hindi (Devanagari) and English (ADR-0008). Old Finworkar stays read-only.

## Implementation and verification

Continue the full 889/45-packet **offline** readiness workflow, preserve fail-closed source/ID/label checks, and enhance smart discovery only with reviewed safe terms and active-category server checks. Design UI options for M-004 approval **before implementing visual screens**. Do not create activation SQL, seed records, claim production-ready coverage, expand public fields, or use Cloudflare staging/remote deployment without the appropriate later approvals. [Release Readiness](../RELEASE_READINESS.md) stays unchecked.

This ADR supersedes any guidance treating the ten reviewed candidates as the required launch limit. It **does not** supersede human review or cloud/device launch gates.
