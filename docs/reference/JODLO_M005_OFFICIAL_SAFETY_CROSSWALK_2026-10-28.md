## Government evidence reviewed

- **Consumer marketplace classification.** [Department of Consumer Affairs, Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/E%20commerce%20rules.pdf): includes certain digitally sold services and marketplace models. Qualified counsel must determine applicability to JODLO's actual lead-only versus paid transaction model, and applicable listing, complaint, grievance and consumer disclosures. Do not assume all obligations or exemptions without that assessment.
- **Data/privacy and transition.** [MeitY Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025, notification dated 13 Nov 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf): phased implementation. Rules 1, 2, 17–21 took effect on Gazette publication; Rule 4 scheduled a year later (13 Nov 2026); Rules 3, 5–16 and 22–23 eighteen months later (13 May 2027). **As of 28 October 2026, do not claim all provisions are in force**. Separate review of Act commencement and later amendments, device access and worker/customer data consent is required.
- **Electrical.** [Central Electricity Authority official notified regulations listing, 2023 and 2026 amendment](https://cea.nic.in/sub-sub-heading-view?lang=en&mappingId=653&subHeadingId=1036&subSubHeadingId=10091): verify current technical safety and applicable state-level electrician/contractor authorizations. Merely appearing in the old catalog is not a credential.
- **Refrigeration/AC.** [MoEFCC Ozone Cell official service training manuals](https://ozonecell.nic.in/home-page/refrigeration-air-conditioning-servicing-sector/page-refrigeration-air-conditioning-manual/): reference specialist handling of refrigerants, equipment and safe procedures. Training documentation is not proof a particular technician holds qualifications.
- **Minors.** [India Code POCSO Act 2012, sections 19–21](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/17804?sam_handle=123456789%2F2517): child safeguarding and offence-reporting provisions need expert review before any home-tutoring contact workflow. Do not invent a universal licence requirement from this Act alone.
